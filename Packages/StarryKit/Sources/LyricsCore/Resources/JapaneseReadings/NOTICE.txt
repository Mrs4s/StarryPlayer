Retained Japanese on/kun readings only; normalized to hiragana and removed
okurigana/affix markers, other languages, meanings and indices. No lyric-specific entries.
See KANJIDIC2/DOCUMENTATION.html, KANJIDIC2/EDRDG-LICENCE.html and
KANJIDIC2/LICENSE-CC-BY-SA-4.0.html.

Purpose flags retain the distinction between sources and uses, including shared readings.

Rebuild with scripts/build-japanese-readings.py using an indexed word snapshot
and kanjidic2.xml.gz from https://www.edrdg.org/kanjidic/.
